Privacy Policy

Languan Okul (grade 7), Languan Okul 8 (grade 8) and Languan Okul 9, 10, 11, 12 (high school) · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy applies to both apps; "the app" below refers to either.

Summary: The app collects no data and sends no personal data off the device. Solving questions needs no internet; the internet is used only to talk to Apple's App Store when the Full Version is bought or restored. There is no sign-in, no account and no registration.

1. Data collected

None. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising network and no third-party SDK. Its only network traffic is Apple's StoreKit talking to the App Store during a purchase (see section 7); nothing is sent to us or to any other server.

2. Stored on the device

The following is kept only in the device's own storage (iOS UserDefaults) and is never transmitted to us, to any server of ours, or to any third party:

StoredPurpose
A first name entered by the user (optional, may be left blank) In-app greeting
Daily question goalDaily progress ring
Per-outcome correct/incorrect countersProgress and learning path
Daily streak and number of questions solved todayStreak and daily goal
Review queue (ids and dates of questions answered incorrectly) Spaced repetition
Points, feathers, quest state, badges earned, accessories Gamification
Per-subject speed-race recordsRacing against one's own record
Date the app was first openedTiming the 24-hour "everything open" period
Short summary for the Home Screen widget (streak, daily goal) — in versions with a widget Showing the widget on the same device

All of it is removed from the device when the app is deleted, and can be reset at any time from the menu on the Progress tab.

3. Network

Solving questions needs no internet. Questions and curriculum data are bundled inside the app; it works in airplane mode. The internet is used only to show the Full Version's price, buy it or restore it through Apple's App Store.

4. Read-aloud

Reading solutions aloud is performed on the device by iOS's own speech synthesiser (AVSpeechSynthesizer). The text read is the solution text inside the app and is not sent to any server.

5. Children

The app is designed for middle-school students. Since no data is collected, no data is collected from children either. There are no accounts, chat, user-generated content, social features, outbound links or advertising in the app.

6. Third parties

No data is sent to any third-party service. The app contains no advertising network, measurement tool or tracking technology. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is not shown because no tracking takes place.

7. Purchases

The app is free to download. For 24 hours after the first launch every subject is open; after that the free subject is Religious Culture in the grade 7 and 8 apps and Geography in the high-school apps (grades 9-12) and the other subjects are unlocked permanently by a one-time in-app purchase called "Full Version" (Tam Sürüm). There is no subscription. Payment is taken entirely by Apple through the App Store; no payment information reaches us or is requested or processed by the app.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is revised. Any update that changes data-collection behaviour will also be stated in the app's release notes.
